Our Partners

Club Sponsors

Tygerberg Cricket Club would not be possible without the generous support of our sponsors. Their commitment to our club enables us to develop players, maintain facilities, and continue building on our proud legacy. We are deeply grateful to each of them.

TW Profile Services
Steel & Engineering

A leading steel service centre operating since 1994, TW Profile Services specialises in laser cutting, profiling, plasma cutting, bending and rolling. With over 300 employees and a reputation for precision craftsmanship, they are one of South Africa's foremost steel service providers.

Optifect Group
Diversified Group of Companies

A Black Empowered diverse group of companies invested in various sectors of economic growth, including steel and engineering, mining and minerals, civils and construction, ICT, and business services. Optifect is committed to transformation and excellence across all industries it operates in.

Visit Website
Future Building Training Consultants
Training & Health

An accredited training and medical service provider based in Brackenfell, Western Cape. FBTC specialises in lifting operations, health and safety, first aid, fire fighting, and workplace medicals. Their vision is simple — to train for a better tomorrow.

Visit Website
M&L Panelbeaters
Automotive Repair

Cape Town's trusted autobody repair centre since 1984. M&L Panelbeaters is manufacturer and insurance approved, offering expert panel beating, spray painting, and wheel alignment from their Goodwood workshop. A family-owned business with over 40 years of service excellence.

Visit Website
Sales Hire
Equipment & Tool Hire

Established in 1990, Sales Hire is a leading builder's equipment and tool hire company with 11 branches and 2 franchise outlets across the Cape Peninsula and Boland. Their services include construction equipment, tool hire, ablution services, and toilet hire — available wherever you need them.
