01

Getting Started

Access is for TCC members. Every new player goes through a quick one-time verification before picking their first team.

01

Hit Enter the League

From the Fantasy home page, tap Enter the League or Pick XI ↗. You'll land on the sign-in screen.

02

Enter your email

A 6-digit one-time code is sent to your inbox. No password — ever. The code expires in 10 minutes, so check your email promptly. Check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive.

03

Set your name and team name

First time only. Your team name is what everyone sees on the leaderboard — pick something good. You can update it later from the pick page.

04

Wait for approval

New registrations are reviewed to keep the league club-member only. You'll receive a confirmation email once you're approved. After that, signing in is instant — just email and code, every time.

02

Picking Your XI

You have R100 to pick 11 players. Every manager gets the same budget — the edge is entirely in how you spend it.

👈

Player Pool

Browse all available players sorted by value. Filter by role — Bat / Bowl / AR / WK — or search by name. Each card shows the player's fantasy value, their form dots over the last 3 rounds, and what percentage of managers have picked them. Low-ownership picks are your differentials.

🏟️

Pitch View

Selected players snap onto a visual pitch divided into four zones — Keeper · Batters · All-Rounders · Bowlers. You can see your team shape at a glance. Tap any chip on the pitch to cycle their state or remove them.

📋

My XI List

A running list of your selected players with values, roles, and captain/VC labels. Shows budget spent and remaining. The Submit button only activates when you have exactly 11 players, a captain, and a vice-captain.

💰

Budget Bar

The gold bar along the top fills as you spend. Players greyed out in the pool would take you over your R100 — drop someone cheaper to free up space.

Your team carries over automatically. You don't need to re-pick every round. When a new round opens, your XI from the previous round is already saved as your team. Just open the pick screen if you want to make changes — otherwise you're already entered. Any changes must be submitted before the deadline to take effect.

Deadline: Each round has a pick deadline shown in the top bar. Once it closes, no changes are allowed. You can swap players, change your captain or vice-captain as many times as you like before then — every submit overwrites your previous selection.

No active round? If there's nothing open, the pick page will say so. Check back closer to the next match day — a new round opens a few days before each fixture.

04 · Power-ups

Chips & Power-ups

Each manager gets four one-time power-ups for the whole season. Play them from the pick screen before a round locks. Choose your moment — once a chip is spent, it's gone until next season.

🚀
Triple CaptainYour captain scores ×3 instead of ×2 for that round. Save it for a round where your captain is a near-certain big scorer.
💪
Bench BoostA flat +25-point safety net added to your round total. Useful in a round where you expect modest returns.
💰
Budget Boost+R25 to your budget for one round, so you can fit in an extra premium player. The boost applies to that round only.
🛡️
Captain Safety NetInsurance against a captain blank — if your captain scores under 10, your vice-captain is promoted to captain (×2) for that round instead.
Triple Captain stacks with wildcard rounds. If you play Triple Captain on a round the admin has already marked ⭐ wildcard, your captain still scores ×3 (the multipliers don't compound beyond that). Track which chips you've used on the Insights page.
05

Round Boosts

Separate from your chips. Boosts are set by the admin for a whole round and apply to everyone — you don't choose them and they don't use up anything. A banner on the pick screen tells you which one is live before you confirm your XI.

🏏
Batters DoubleBatting points ×2 for batters and keepers. Load up on top-order players.
🎯
Bowlers DoubleBowling points and economy bonuses ×2 for bowlers. Wicket-takers become very valuable.
⚡
All-Rounders BoostBatting and bowling points ×1.5 for all-rounders. The round to pack your side with them.
📉
Economy Bonus WeekendEconomy bonuses ×2. Tight, disciplined bowlers outscore expensive strike bowlers.
🏆
POTM DoubleThe Player of the Match bonus ×2. Backing the likely match-winner pays off twice over.
Boosts and chips stack. Playing Triple Captain on a Batters Double round with a top-order captain is about as good as it gets — the boost doubles their batting, then the captaincy triples the total. Check the banner before you lock in.

Round Boosts

Some rounds carry a boost modifier that amplifies points in a specific category. The active boost is shown on the pick screen and the leaderboard. There are five types:

🏏
Batters DoubleAll batting points (runs + milestone bonuses) are doubled for this round.
🎳
Bowlers DoubleAll bowling points (wickets, maidens, 5-fer bonus) are doubled for this round.
⚡
All-Rounders BoostPlayers who contribute with both bat and ball receive a bonus on their combined tally.
💨
Economy BonusEconomy rate points (positive and negative) are doubled — tight bowling is worth even more.
⭐
POTM DoubleThe Player of the Match bonus doubles from 25 pt to 50 pt. Picking the standout performer is huge.

Boosts stack with captaincy. If it's a Bowlers Double round and your captain takes 3 wickets, the doubled bowling points are then multiplied again by ×2 (or ×3 on a wildcard). Always pick your captain with the active boost in mind.

07

Stay In the Loop

Two ways the league keeps you across what's happening — no need to keep refreshing the app.

🔔

Deadline Reminders

On the pick page, tap the bell icon (🔕) in the top-right corner. You'll get a push notification 24 hours before the deadline and again 1 hour before it closes. The bell turns to 🔔 when active — tap again to turn it off at any time.

📧

Scores Published

After each match, when scores go live, every player who submitted a team that round gets an email with a link straight to the leaderboard. No opt-in needed — it's automatic.

🔒

Picks Locked

When the deadline passes and the round locks, everyone who submitted a team gets a confirmation email — your XI is set, sit back and watch the points come in. You'll also receive an email when the H2H draw is generated (showing your opponent) and again when results are resolved (showing the final score, margin, and your W/D/L).

On iPhone? Push notifications require the app on your Home Screen first. In Safari, tap the Share button then Add to Home Screen. Open the app from there, then tap the bell to subscribe. On Android Chrome it works directly in the browser.

08

The Leaderboard

Track the standings, dig into how everyone scored, and see which players were most picked.

🏆

Season Total

The leaderboard loads with cumulative points across all rounds. The number of rounds each manager has participated in is shown alongside their total.

🔍

Round Filter

Click any round button at the top to see that round's scores in isolation — useful for seeing who moved up or down and where you gained or lost ground.

▸

Score Breakdown

Click any row to expand it. You'll see a round-by-round history chart and a full per-player breakdown with the exact calculation shown — e.g. "45 runs = 45 pt · 50+ bonus = +10 pt · Captain ×2 = 110 pt total".

🏅

Player of the Round

When viewing a specific round, the highest-scoring player across all teams is highlighted. A useful indicator for future captain picks.

📊

Most Picked Players

Shows the most popular picks that round with a percentage bar. Low-ownership players who score big are the differentials that move you up the table.

🔴

Live Tab

On match days, a 🔴 Live tab appears on the leaderboard. It shows real-time points calculated from the live scoring feed — so you can watch your score update ball by ball as the game is played. Only active during rounds linked to a live fixture.

⚔️

H2H Tab

When Head-to-Head rounds have been played, a second ⚔ H2H tab appears alongside the points view. It shows the season W/D/L standings table and the full fixture grid for each H2H round — who faced who and what the final scores were.

Share your XI: After submitting, hit 📲 Share XI on the success screen to get a trading-card graphic of your pitch layout with captain and VC highlighted. Share directly to WhatsApp or Instagram Stories, or download as a PNG.

09

Head-to-Head Rounds

Some rounds are designated as Head-to-Head. Instead of just accumulating points, you go head-to-head against one other manager — your XI vs theirs, same players, same scores. Whoever scores more wins the matchup.

How it works

⚔️

Round flag

H2H is a round type, not a permanent mode. Look for the ⚔ marker on the round name when you're on the pick page — this tells you it's a H2H round before the deadline.

🎲

Fixture draw

Pairings are generated automatically. In the early rounds the draw is random. Once at least 3 rounds have been scored, the draw switches to standings-based — 1st plays 2nd, 3rd plays 4th, and so on — so tougher matchups come as the season develops.

👻

Mr. Average (odd numbers)

If there's an odd number of participants, one manager gets matched against Mr. Average — a ghost opponent whose score equals the mean of all real managers that round. You still have to beat the group to win the matchup, so there are no free wins.

🏷️

Opponent banner

When you open the pick screen on a H2H round, a banner below the deadline tells you who you're facing: ⚔ H2H Round · You vs [Team Name]. This is visible as soon as fixtures have been generated — use it to inform your captain choice.

📨

Draw email

When the fixture draw is generated, you'll receive an email showing your opponent and the picks deadline. No need to check the app — your matchup comes straight to your inbox.

📊

Result email

After the round is resolved, you'll get a result email showing your score vs your opponent's, the margin, whether you won, drew, or lost, and your updated season H2H record (e.g. W2 D0 L1).

Points & standings

🏆
Win
3 pts
Your XI outscores your opponent's this round. Three H2H league points.
🤝
Draw
1 pt
Identical scores. One H2H point each — rare, but it happens.
📉
Loss
0 pts
Your opponent scored more this round. No H2H points, but your season total still grows.

H2H points are separate from season points. Your fantasy total keeps accumulating as normal across all rounds. The H2H standings use a separate W/D/L table (like a football league) where ties are broken by total fantasy points scored across H2H rounds.

H2H standings on the leaderboard

Switch to the ⚔ H2H tab on the leaderboard to see the H2H league table. Columns show W, D, L, H2H points, and fantasy points — sorted by H2H points with fantasy points as the tiebreaker. Scroll down for a round-by-round fixture grid with the exact scores for every matchup.

Strategy

🎯

Know your opponent before picking

Check who you're facing before the deadline. If your opponent is likely to captain a big hitter, back someone with similar or higher ceiling. A safe 30-point captain pick might lose you the matchup even if it was the right call in a normal round.

⚡

Differentials hit harder in H2H

Picking a low-ownership player who goes big doesn't just move you up the season table — it wins you the matchup outright. A differential 80-pt knock beats a chalk 60-pt one every time.

🧮

One win beats two draws

In the H2H table a win (3 pts) beats two draws (1 pt each). Back yourself to outscore your opponent — don't play it safe just to avoid losing.

Team of the Week

After every round, a balanced XI is picked from the best real performances — not from anyone's fantasy squad. It goes up on the leaderboard as a team sheet, with the round's standout circled.

5
BattersThe five highest-scoring batters of the round, keeper-batters included.
1
WicketkeeperMarked † on the sheet, as on a real scorecard.
2
All-roundersPlayers who contributed with both bat and ball.
3
BowlersRanked on the same points the league uses, so wickets and economy both count.
Why the top scorer isn't always first. Because the side has to be balanced, a keeper on 62 can take his slot ahead of a bowler on 85 — the bowler still appears, just in a bowling place. If a round hasn't enough specialists in a role, the next best performer fills in and is marked filler so it's clear why they're there. A 12th man is named below the XI.

Achievement Badges

Earn badges for standout performances, hot streaks, and podium finishes. Your badges are shown on the leaderboard and on your dedicated badges page.

🔥

Hot Streak

Score above the season average in 5 consecutive rounds. Consistency is rewarded.

🏆

Podium Hat-Trick

Finish in the top 3 in 3 consecutive rounds. Staying at the top is harder than getting there.

⭐

Century Captain

Your captain scores 100+ pts in a single round. Back the right player on the right day.

🎯

Sharp Eye

Pick the Player of the Round 3 times across the season. The best differential pick at the right moment.

💎

Diamond Pick

Pick a player who scores 100+ pts in a single round. Find the diamond in the rough.

⚡

All-Rounder

Have a player from all 4 roles score 20+ pts in the same round. A perfectly balanced team.

🏏

Wicket Machine

Have 3+ bowlers take wickets in the same round. Load up on bowlers and let them rip.

🦁

Season Survivor

Submit a team in every single round of the season without missing one. Show up every week.

Viewing your badges: Head to the Badges page to see your earned badges, track progress towards in-progress ones, and browse everything that's locked. Earned badges also appear as small icons next to your name on the leaderboard.
