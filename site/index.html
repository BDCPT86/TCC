Who We Are

About Tygerberg CC

Founded in 1957 in Tiervlei — now Ravensmead — Tygerberg Cricket Club has grown from humble beginnings into one of the Western Cape's most respected cricket institutions. Built on resilience, community spirit, and a commitment to development, we have produced top provincial and international talent while playing a proud role in South Africa's cricketing history.

In 1992 we were among the first clubs in the country to unite across racial lines, merging with Parow Cricket Club. Over the decades TCC has produced players who have represented Western Province, South African Women, and the national side — a testament to the club's dedication to developing talent at every level.

Next Generation

Youth & Junior Cricket

Tygerberg CC's junior programme is central to the club's identity. With 70 junior members across five age groups, we are one of the most active youth cricket clubs in the Northern Suburbs of Cape Town.

All sessions are held at Florida Park, Ravensmead under qualified coaches. Equipment is available for new players — just arrive ready to play and learn.
