Club Membership

TCC has been part of the Florida Park and Ravensmead community since 1957. Whether you're a seasoned cricketer, a junior just starting out, or simply want to support your local club — there's a place for you at Tygerberg.

The easiest way to join is to come down to training on Tuesday or Thursday evenings at Florida Park. You'll meet the team, have a hit in the nets, and get sorted from there. If you'd like to make contact first, use the button below.

How to join

01

Come to training

The simplest way. Rock up at Florida Park on a Tuesday or Thursday evening from 17:30. No appointment needed.

02

Or get in touch first

Use our contact form and let us know you're interested. Someone from the club will get back to you shortly.

03

Pay your fees

Fees are paid at the clubhouse or via EFT. The club secretary will provide banking details on registration.

04

You're in

Once registered and paid, you're a TCC member — welcome to the family. Facta Non Verba.

Ready to join?

Come down to training or send us a message — we'd love to have you.
